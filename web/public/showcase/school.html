Admissions

How to Apply

A clear, three-step process. No ambiguity, no hidden requirements.

1

Submit an Inquiry

Fill out the online inquiry form with your child's details. We respond within 2 working days with the application pack and fee structure.

2

Campus Visit & Interaction

Schedule a campus tour. Your child will have a brief, friendly interaction with our admissions team — not a stress test, just a conversation.

3

Admission Decision

You'll hear back within 5 working days. If offered a seat, you'll have 10 days to confirm enrollment and complete the documentation.
