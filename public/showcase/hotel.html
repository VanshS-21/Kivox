Check-in: 2:00 PM · Check-out: 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out subject to availability (complimentary for suite guests).
Cancellation
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival. After that, one night's stay will be charged. No-show: full stay charged.
Payment
All major credit cards accepted. 50% advance at booking, balance at check-in. GST included in all listed rates.
Pet Policy
Small pets welcome in select rooms (under 10kg). ₹2,000/night surcharge. Service animals always welcome.
