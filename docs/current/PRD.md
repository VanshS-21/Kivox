# Kivox Website PRD (Pre-Code)

## 1) Purpose

Build a premium, craft-forward studio website that converts qualified SMB owners/operators into serious project inquiries.

This PRD defines what must be decided and prepared **before writing any code**.

## 2) Product Principles

- **Clarity → action:** visitors quickly understand what Kivox does and what to do next.
- **Craft is the proof:** the site itself must feel premium, fast, and considered.
- **No generic agency language:** plain, confident copy; minimal jargon; concrete examples.
- **Truthful proof:** use studio demonstrations and process artifacts until real client work exists.

## 3) Target User

**Primary buyer:** SMB owner/operator.

Typical situations:

- Launching a new business or new location
- Frustrated with an outdated/unclear website
- Losing inquiries because information is hard to find
- Needs a booking/appointment/inquiry workflow, not just a brochure site

## 4) Goals & Success Metrics

### 4.1 Primary Goal

- Increase **qualified** project inquiries.

### 4.2 Secondary Goals

- Establish premium trust through design and clarity.
- Make services easy to understand (without packages).
- Create a foundation for future SEO pages (services + industries + work).

### 4.3 Success Signals (Launch)

- Inquiry volume and quality trend upward.
- Visitors reach the Work and Contact sections at a healthy rate.
- Form completion rate is acceptable and improves over iterations.

## 5) Scope (Launch Version)

### 5.1 Pages

- **Home**
- **Work** (studio demonstrations)
- **Contact**
- **Privacy**
- **Terms**

### 5.2 Navigation

Keep navigation minimal and obvious:

- Work
- Services
- Process
- Contact

## 6) Homepage Information Architecture (Recommended)

This is designed to borrow “work-forward + confidence” patterns from madebycat.com while keeping Kivox’s story truthful and SMB-readable.

1. **Hero**
   - Headline: “Make your business easier to trust online.”
   - Subhead with outcomes + capabilities
   - CTAs: Start a project / View studio work
2. **What We Do (Services)**
   - Services in plain language with “when you need this”
3. **Work (Studio Demonstrations)**
   - A small grid preview linking to Work page
4. **Process**
   - Short, confident sequence (discover → structure → design → build → launch)
5. **Trust Details**
   - Quality signals without “defensive disclaimers”
   - Examples: performance-first, accessibility, clear handover, analytics-ready (optional)
6. **Contact**
   - Short form + direct email option

## 7) Work (Studio Demonstrations) Requirements

### 7.1 Work Types

Initial set (confirmed):

- Hospital website / patient journey clarity (studio demonstration)
- Cafe / menu + visit intent (studio demonstration)
- Hotel / booking confidence (studio demonstration)
- School / admissions clarity (studio demonstration)
- Fitness / wellness / schedule + inquiry clarity (studio demonstration)

### 7.2 Work Page Template (Per Project)

Each studio demonstration should include:

- One-line “what this demonstrates”
- A few key screens
- A short list of decisions (structure, content hierarchy, UX logic)
- A short list of what was built (public-facing, no unnecessary tech flexing)
- CTA to contact

## 8) Services (Offer) Requirements

Services to present (confirmed):

- Websites & web apps
- UI/UX redesign
- SEO & AI search readiness
- Brand identity
- Backend-enabled systems (select)
- Android apps (select)

### 8.1 Service Copy Rules

- Do not present as “packages.”
- Always include “when this is the right fit.”
- Avoid promising rankings, leads, revenue, or outcomes you can’t guarantee.

## 9) Contact / Inquiry Requirements

### 9.1 Inquiry Form Objectives

- Collect enough info to reply with a useful next step.
- Keep friction low for serious buyers.
- Route inquiries by service and industry.

### 9.2 Form Fields (Recommended)

Required:

- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Business type (select)
- What you need (select: Website / Web app / Redesign / SEO / Brand / Backend-enabled system / Android app)
- Primary goal (short text or select)

Optional:

- Current website (URL)
- Timeline (select)
- Notes (textarea)

### 9.3 Post-Submit Behavior

- Confirmation message that sets expectations (reply within 24 hours + what happens next).
- Auto-reply email (optional) acknowledging receipt.

### 9.4 Spam & Abuse Controls (Pre-Code Requirements)

- Honeypot field
- Minimum submit time
- Server-side validation
- Add stronger protection (Turnstile/recaptcha) only if spam becomes real

## 10) SEO Requirements (Pre-Code)

### 10.1 Launch SEO

- Clear entity information: Kivox, based in Bengaluru, serving India-wide.
- Crawlable service and industry language in visible text.
- Clean title/description; no keyword stuffing.
- Sitemap + robots.

### 10.2 Expansion Plan (Post-Launch, Not Built Yet)

Prepare for (don’t publish thin pages):

- Services pages
- Industry pages
- Work index + work detail pages (as demos mature into real proof)
- Resource pages answering buyer questions

## 11) Analytics & Privacy Requirements (Pre-Code)

### 11.1 What We Track

Intent and funnel behavior:

- CTA clicks
- Work page interactions
- Form started / submitted / failed
- Service interest selection

### 11.2 What We Never Track

- Names, emails, phone numbers, URLs, or free-text notes as analytics properties.

### 11.3 Privacy Pages

- Explain what data is collected via inquiry form.
- Explain analytics at a high level (if used).

## 12) Content Requirements Checklist (Before Build)

- Final homepage hero headline + subhead.
- Final service blurbs (one-liners + when-needed bullets).
- Studio demonstration writeups + images/screens.
- Contact routing details (real email/phone).
- Privacy + terms copy (placeholder acceptable initially).

## 13) Acceptance Criteria (Definition of Ready to Build)

- Brand strategy and PRD are approved.
- Copy is finalized for hero/services/contact.
- At least 3 studio demonstrations are ready to publish.
- Inquiry form fields and routing are finalized.
- Legal placeholders are ready (privacy/terms).
- SEO posture is agreed (no thin pages, no overclaims).
