# Hotel Website (Studio Demonstration)

## What This Demonstrates

Booking confidence: clear rooms, policies, and direct inquiry/booking paths with premium presentation.

## Target Audience

- Couples/families comparing stays
- Business travelers checking location and reliability
- Guests who need policy clarity before booking

## Primary Actions

- Explore rooms and amenities
- Check policies (check-in, cancellation, etc.)
- Book / inquire

## Core Pages / Sections

- Home (value + room preview + location)
- Rooms (list + detail pages)
- Amenities
- Policies
- Contact / Booking inquiry

## Key UX Decisions

- Room details prioritized over generic marketing copy.
- Booking/inquiry path visible without forcing third-party OTAs.
- Trust signals through clarity: policies, photos, and location info.

## Proof Notes

- Label: Studio Demonstration
- No real hotel branding or claim of affiliation.
